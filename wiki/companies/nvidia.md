# Nvidia
- CEO Jensen Huang resigned after 33 years; CFO Colette Kress became interim CEO. [SPN-1112-02, 2026-11-12]
- Nvidia reported record Q3 revenue of US$115.8 billion, more than double the prior year, led by data center. [SPN-1120-07, 2026-11-20]
- It raised its forecast and says it can thrive without Huang, rejecting his return. [SPN-1119-07, 2026-11-19]
- It is securing memory, substrate and advanced packaging capacity, including a US$1 billion-plus investment in Kinsus and a packaging deal with SPIL. [SPN-1118-04, 2026-11-18]
- The business impact of the leadership change is covered in [[events/nvidia-leadership-change-2026-11]].
