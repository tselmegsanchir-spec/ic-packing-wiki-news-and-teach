# TSMC
- TSMC set up a US$500 million investment company to take stakes in chip-ecosystem firms. [SPN-1112-08, 2026-11-12]
- It bought a 0.03% stake in Anthropic for US$500 million at the US$83 IPO price. [SPN-1120-02, 2026-11-20]
- It received NT$167.586 billion in subsidies in the first nine months of 2026. [SPN-1116-05, 2026-11-16]
- It halted advanced-node orders (below 16 nm) from Chinese chipmakers lacking US licences. [SPN-1120-04, 2026-11-20]
