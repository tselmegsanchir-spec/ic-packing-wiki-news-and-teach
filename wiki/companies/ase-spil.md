# ASE and SPIL (Taiwan OSATs)
- ASE is investing NT$100 billion in two Kaohsiung facilities for advanced AI chip packaging. [SPN-1116-02, 2026-11-16]
- SPIL partnered with Nvidia to triple Taiwan's advanced packaging capacity by mid-2029. [SPN-1119-01, 2026-11-19]
- (Explainer) Both are OSATs, the companies that package and test chips for foundries and chip designers.
