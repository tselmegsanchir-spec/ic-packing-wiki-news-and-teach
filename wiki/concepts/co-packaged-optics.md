# Optics and photonics in packaging
Summary: Light is moving closer to the chip because copper links are limited by power and reach.

- Credo showed a 1.6T active light cable with copper-level reliability over 50 m and lower power at OCP 2026. [SPN-1112-06, 2026-11-12]
- Optical computing is gaining momentum as AI inference demands energy efficiency, with startups building photonic chips. [SPN-1118-08, 2026-11-18]
- imec will open a Japan R&D base at Hokkaido University with 15 researchers over five years on photonic ICs and packaging. [SPN-1118-11, 2026-11-18]
- Detachable fiber connectors for co-packaged and near-packaged optics face challenges in standardization, assembly and alignment. [SPN-1120-11, 2026-11-20]
- Photonic design must be evaluated at system level because the combined optical link can differ from individual parts. [SPN-1125-10, 2026-11-25]
