# Advanced packaging capacity
Summary: Packaging has become a bottleneck for AI chips, and companies are adding capacity in Taiwan, the US and India.

- ASE is investing NT$100 billion in two Kaohsiung facilities for advanced AI chip packaging, aiming at NT$80 billion annual revenue and 5,000 jobs at full capacity. [SPN-1116-02, 2026-11-16]
- SPIL partnered with Nvidia to triple Taiwan's advanced packaging capacity by mid-2029. [SPN-1119-01, 2026-11-19]
- Nvidia is investing over US$1 billion in Kinsus to expand substrate manufacturing. [SPN-1119-01, 2026-11-19]
- Intel named Microsoft as its first customer for EMIB advanced packaging. [SPN-1120-01, 2026-11-20]
- India is expanding IC packaging through joint ventures and facilities by Vishay, Kaynes Technology, Paras Semiconductors and BroadPak. [SPN-1118-01, 2026-11-18]
- A market-research release forecasts the OSAT market at US$80.2 billion by 2033. [SPN-1118-02, 2026-11-18]
- (Explainer) OSAT means outsourced semiconductor assembly and test: companies like ASE and SPIL package chips that foundries make.
