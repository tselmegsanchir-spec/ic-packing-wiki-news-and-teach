# AI agents in chip design
Summary: Several issues argue that AI agents are entering the EDA flow, with open problems around trust, data and cost.

- Agentic AI automates semiconductor engineering workflows and widens design exploration while engineers keep oversight and verification. [SPN-1113-10, 2026-11-13]
- AI agents automate customization, verification and optimization, but orchestration, security, data privacy and reliability must improve before agents get more autonomy. [SPN-1117-01, 2026-11-17]
- Synopsys, Hiveware, Silvaco and Axiomise discuss integration, preserving engineering expertise, protecting proprietary data and controlling cost. [SPN-1119-10, 2026-11-19]
- Siemens will acquire Lauterbach to combine chip verification with software debugging in its EDA portfolio, aiming for earlier software testing. [SPN-1112-11, 2026-11-12]
- (Explainer) "Shift left" means finding problems earlier in the design flow, for example verifying software on a model of the chip before silicon exists.
- (AI hypothesis) If agents handle routine verification and exploration, a junior designer's value may move toward writing good specifications and judging results; no source says this.
