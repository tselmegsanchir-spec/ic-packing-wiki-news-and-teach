# US AI chip export controls
Summary: November 2026 brought tighter rules, and companies adjusted shipping and order policies.

- The US requires licences for overseas data centres serving Chinese firms, closing a cloud-computing loophole. [SPN-1117-06, 2026-11-17]
- TrendForce says the new rules tighten compliance for Taiwan's foundries, packaging firms and AI suppliers and may delay orders. [SPN-1119-02, 2026-11-19]
- TSMC halted advanced manufacturing orders below 16 nm from Chinese chipmakers without US licences. [SPN-1120-04, 2026-11-20]
- Nvidia stopped shipping licensed Hopper AI chips to China. [SPN-1119-05, 2026-11-19]
- Nvidia warned customers of legal risks and plans to restrict renting GPUs to Chinese businesses. [SPN-1118-12, 2026-11-18]
- Nvidia formed a board committee on national security and export controls. [SPN-1118-07, 2026-11-18]
- The US House Speaker cited growing consensus for tighter curbs. [SPN-1116-07, 2026-11-16]
