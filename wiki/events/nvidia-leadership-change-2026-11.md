# Nvidia leadership change, Nov 2026 (business impact only)
Scope note: personal-allegation details are intentionally left out; only supply-chain and market effects are recorded.

- Huang resigned and the stock fell 13%. [SPN-1112-02, 2026-11-12]
- Samsung and SK hynix expect existing supply agreements to stay largely unaffected. [SPN-1112-05, 2026-11-12]
- AMD and Broadcom shares rose as investors expected customers to diversify suppliers. [SPN-1113-06, 2026-11-13]
- Experts expect little disruption for Taiwan's supply chain, noting strong supplier ties and the entrenched CUDA platform. [SPN-1116-08, 2026-11-16]
- Analysts see no immediate disruption for TSMC, Samsung and ASUS, but long-term roadmap concerns remain. [SPN-1116-10, 2026-11-16]
- Taiwanese economists warn of possible supply-chain reshaping and squeezed server-maker margins. [SPN-1119-06, 2026-11-19]
- Academia Sinica experts see the change as part of US-China supply-chain restructuring. [SPN-1119-08, 2026-11-19]
- Update: Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO after briefly removing him; shares rose 25% premarket. [SPN-1124-05, 2026-11-24]
- An editorial argues that government stakes in firms bias US export-control decisions. [SPN-1123-05, 2026-11-23]
- Analysts say the CEO change plus export scrutiny and rare-earth shortages add supply-chain uncertainty; Southeast Asian data-center growth still faces limits. [SPN-1125-13, 2026-11-25] [SPN-1125-06, 2026-11-25]
