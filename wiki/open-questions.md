# Open questions and conflicts
## Conflict 1: TSMC investment amounts
- One report says TSMC set up a US$500 million investment company. [SPN-1112-08, 2026-11-12]
- Another says TSMC invests up to US$1 billion in OpenAI and Anthropic. [SPN-1112-09, 2026-11-12]
- A later report says TSMC paid US$500 million for a 0.03% Anthropic stake. [SPN-1120-02, 2026-11-20]
- Status: both recorded, not reconciled; the reports may describe different things (fund vs. specific stakes).
