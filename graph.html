Graph and loop design

How information is acquired, organised, checked and turned into the lesson and briefing.

The graph

Course chain: obtain information, consult the wiki, summarise key points, check, generate. Here "obtain" = Read mail to Tier + notes; "consult the wiki" = Wiki pages to Pick stories; "summarise" = Write cards; "check" = the gates; "generate" = Render and Deliver.

Which steps repeat?

The four gates repeat. Lint, Faithfulness, Consistency and Render gate each run again after a fix; Faithfulness and Render are capped at 3 passes. The Outline gate repeats until the owner approves. A failed gate returns to the step just before it, never all the way to the start.

Which parts must be checked or regenerated?

Checked by script: wiki citations and links (Lint), numbers and names in each fact against its wiki card (Faithfulness), Mongolian numbers and names against the English cards (Consistency), overflow, broken links, stat values and phone/desktop layout (Render gate). Regenerated: only the cards or the page that failed. Wiki pages are append-only and are not regenerated.

Why this design?

Facts must trace to the wiki, so every fact carries an ID and each step sees only its allowed input. Cheap script checks run on every pass; the expensive human check happens once, at the outline, where a wrong direction costs the least. Gates sit right after the step that tends to fail, so an error is found where it was made.
